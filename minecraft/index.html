Frequently Asked Questions

What are the new mobs added in Minecraft 1.21 Tricky Trials?
Minecraft 1.21 (Tricky Trials) added the Breeze (a hostile whirlwind mob inhabiting Trial Chambers that attacks using wind charges) and the Armadillo (a passive mob found in savanna and badlands biomes that curls into a protective ball and drops Armadillo Scutes used to craft Wolf Armor).
What is the strongest hostile mob in Minecraft 1.21?
The Warden is the strongest non-boss mob in Minecraft with 500 HP (250 hearts) and up to 45 melee attack damage, capable of eliminating fully-armored players in a single hit. For boss mobs, the Wither has 300 HP and the Ender Dragon has 200 HP.
What does the Breeze drop in Minecraft 1.21?
Defeating the Breeze drops 1–2 Breeze Rods, which are essential ingredients used to craft the heavy-smash Mace weapon and throwable Wind Charges.
How do you craft Wolf Armor and protect wolves?
Wolf Armor is crafted using 6 Armadillo Scutes arranged on a crafting table. It can be dyed into custom colors and equipped onto tamed wolves to absorb incoming damage from attacks, explosions, and hazards.
